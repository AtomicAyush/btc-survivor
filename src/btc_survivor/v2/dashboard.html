How to read this page

Runs. S1 trades on prices alone with its news inputs pinned at zero. S2 adds the two news inputs. Both share one feed, one market layer and identical bots at birth, so later differences come from news.

Market session. Bots act only in the regular session, opening from 10:00 to 15:25 ET and flat by 15:55 ET. Nights, weekends and holidays add no minutes.

Feed. Finnhub trade prints from the free feed, a partial tape that carries only part of each symbol's trades. A symbol goes stale when no eligible print comes for longer than the limit the run config sets from the gate session's gaps, and stale or recovering pauses everything on it.

News. The news process writes a heartbeat every 30 seconds. Without a fresh healthy heartbeat both runs pause rent, learning and decisions. Model spend has one cap of 20 dollars a month, and a day above a quarter of it raises an alert that stops nothing.

Skill. The correlation of each predictor with the next 30 minute return, pooled over the eight symbols from session 2. Everything here is descriptive until the cutoff, and only the news predictor in S2 is ever tested. At the cutoff its pass or fail word here is the first test, and evaluate runs the second, news beyond the price features, only after a pass.

Populations. 24 learners per run, three per home symbol, each with a coin flip twin. Learner against twin separates skill from luck. There is no margin over cost, so a flat learner opens on its own whenever its prediction clears the round trip cost, and the forced timer opens a trade for a flat learner after the configured healthy session hours without one, 3 in the runs and half an hour in the rehearsal.

Holding. By the owner's decision of 13 October 2026 a learner holding a position has already paid to enter and pays to exit whenever it leaves, so it keeps the position while its prediction still points the position's way by more than the cost of holding it 30 more minutes, nothing for a long and the borrow for a short. It flips only when the prediction points the other way by more than the full required edge of the new side, and otherwise closes. Stops, the 15:55 close and the flattens still end a held position.

Decisions. Every decision of both runs, newest first. A learner decides every 30 session minutes on its home symbol, and its twin copies opens, closes and flips with a coin for the side, keeping its own position while the learner keeps its. A hold keeps the position with no order, and its reason names the holding cost the prediction beat. The required edge and the gate are always the full edge an open needs, which a flip must pass. Choose a row to see its prediction, the edge it needed, its fill and costs, and its label once the 30 minutes have passed.

Freshness. The overview refreshes every 10 seconds and the newest page of decisions every 30, from views the server recomputes at most every 5 seconds.

Times. The runs keep UTC, and so does this page until you choose Local at the top. Local shows every time here in this computer's time zone with its abbreviation, times quoted inside feed events included, and the from and to filters of the decisions then read and show times in that zone too. Times marked ET are New York time, the market's clock, in either choice. A time ending in Z is read as UTC in either zone, so one copied from a log or from status can be pasted as it is, and an hour that repeats when the clocks go back is shown that way. The choice is remembered in this browser.
