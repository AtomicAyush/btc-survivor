How to read this page

Runs. S1 trades on prices alone with its news inputs pinned at zero. S2 adds the two news inputs. Both share one feed, one market layer and identical bots at birth, so later differences come from news.

Market session. Bots act only in the regular session, opening from 10:00 to 15:25 ET and flat by 15:55 ET. Nights, weekends and holidays add no minutes.

Feed. Finnhub trade prints. A symbol is healthy while it prints within 15 seconds, and stale or recovering pauses everything on it.

News. The news process writes a heartbeat every 30 seconds. Without a fresh healthy heartbeat both runs pause rent, learning and decisions. Model spend has one cap of 20 dollars a month, and a day above a quarter of it raises an alert that stops nothing.

Skill. The correlation of each predictor with the next 30 minute return, pooled over the eight symbols from session 2. Everything here is descriptive until the cutoff, and only the news predictor in S2 is ever tested.

Populations. 24 learners per run, three per home symbol, each with a coin flip twin. Learner against twin separates skill from luck.

Decisions. Every decision of both runs, newest first. A learner decides every 30 session minutes on its home symbol, and its twin copies opens, closes and flips with a coin for the side. Choose a row to see its prediction, the edge it needed, its fill and costs, and its label once the 30 minutes have passed.

Freshness. The overview refreshes every 10 seconds and the newest page of decisions every 30, from views the server recomputes at most every 5 seconds. Times are UTC unless marked ET.
